### About section

```
Diabeaters is a companion app for everyday Type 1 diabetes life.

Living with Type 1 means constant decisions — supplies to reorder, meals to plan, exercise to prep for, travel to pack for, and the mental load of getting it right day after day. Diabeaters helps reduce that load with practical tools in one calm, modern app.

What you can do in Diabeaters:
• Supply tracker — stock levels and run-out estimates for prescriptions and kit
• Scenario guides — exercise, sick day, travel, bedtime, alcohol, driving, and more
• Tools — meal & ratios, correction helper, hypo help, routines, appointments
• Community — feed, topics, DMs, and daily check-ins with people who get it
• Supporters — optional read-only access for parents, partners, and carers, with scoped sharing and alerts you control

Diabeaters is built by PassingTime Ltd for people living with Type 1 and the people who support them. We focus on organisation, planning, and peer connection — not replacing your diabetes team.

Download: [APP_STORE] · [PLAY_STORE]
Website: [WEBSITE]
Support: [SUPPORT_EMAIL]

Diabeaters provides general lifestyle organisation for people living with Type 1 diabetes. It does not provide medical advice and is not a medical device. Always follow guidance from your healthcare professional.
```

Why we’re building Diabeaters

Type 1 diabetes doesn’t clock off. Supplies run low at the worst time. Travel needs a backup plan. Exercise needs fueling prep. A sick day changes everything. And most apps either feel clinical, or social, or scattered — rarely all the practical stuff in one place.

Diabeaters is our attempt at a calmer daily flow:

• Open a guide when life gets complicated
